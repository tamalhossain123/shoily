Where we deliver

We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh, with cash on delivery everywhere. Orders are dispatched from our Dhaka studio through trusted couriers including Steadfast and Pathao Courier.

Delivery time

  • Inside Dhaka: 24 to 48 hours.
  • Outside Dhaka: 2 to 4 working days.
  • Custom Studio pieces are made to order and may take 1 to 2 extra days before dispatch.

Delivery charges

  • Order any 2 t-shirts or clothing items and home delivery is completely free, anywhere in Bangladesh.
  • For single-item orders, a standard courier charge applies and is confirmed before dispatch.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we send tracking details by SMS and WhatsApp so you can follow it to your door.

Size exchange

Wrong size? No problem. Message us on WhatsApp at 01567997270 within 7 days of delivery, keep the item unworn with tags on, and we will exchange it for the right size. Exchanges are free inside Dhaka; a courier charge may apply outside Dhaka.

Returns and refunds

  • If your item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we will replace it or refund you in full.
  • Refunds are sent through the same method you paid with (bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank), or as store credit for cash-on-delivery orders, within 5 working days of receiving the item back.
  • For change-of-mind returns, the item must be unused, unwashed and with tags on; delivery charges are not refundable.

Need help?

WhatsApp 01567997270 or email eshoily.net@gmail.com, every day from 10am to 9pm.
