Information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect only what we need to serve you:

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address, so we can deliver your order.
  • Order details: the products, sizes and quantities you buy.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, email or this website, so we can help you.
  • Basic device and browsing information, used to keep the store secure and improve it.

How we use your information

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders anywhere in Bangladesh.
  • To handle size exchanges, returns and support questions.
  • To send order updates by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To improve our products and store experience.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

Payments

Most orders are paid by cash on delivery. If you pay in advance with bKash, Nagad or Rocket, your payment is processed by that provider under their own privacy policy. We never see or store your PIN or full payment credentials.

Sharing with delivery partners

We share your name, phone number and address with our courier partners (such as Steadfast and Pathao Courier) only so they can deliver your parcel and collect payment.

Cookies

Our store uses a small number of cookies to remember your cart and keep you signed in. You can clear them any time from your browser settings.
