About Us

The story behind Shoily, and the standard every single piece has to meet before it reaches you.

Our Story

Shoily started with a simple frustration: clothes that looked great online but disappointed in real life. Thin fabric, prints that faded in two washes, sizes that never matched the chart.

So we decided to make the t-shirt we always wanted to buy. Heavyweight 240+ GSM combed cotton, bio-washed so it never shrinks, and prints that stay sharp wash after wash. Every piece is checked by hand before it leaves our Dhaka studio.

Today Shoily delivers across all 64 districts of Bangladesh, with cash on delivery and a size exchange promise we actually keep. Your Reliable Style Partner is not just a tagline, it is how we run the shop.
