How To Style Heavyweight Tees

Oct 12, 2026 · Style Guide · 4 min read

Start With The Fit

A heavyweight tee has structure, so fit matters more than with a thin shirt. Go true to size for a clean everyday look, or one size up if you want that relaxed, boxy silhouette. The 240+ GSM fabric drapes on the body instead of clinging, which is exactly what makes it look premium.

Three Looks That Always Work

First, the classic: white tee, dark jeans, clean sneakers. Second, layer it under an open overshirt with chinos for evenings. Third, tuck the front into tailored trousers and add a belt, the weight of the fabric keeps the tuck in place all day.

Care Keeps It Sharp

Wash inside out in cold water and dry in shade. Shoily prints are tested for 50+ washes, and bio-washed cotton means the size you buy is the size that stays. Treat it right and a heavyweight tee will outlast five fast-fashion ones.
